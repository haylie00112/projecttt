Family: Liliaceae

Genus: Lilium

Species: Around 100

All About Lilies

Lilies are large, prominent perennials known for their striking trumpet shapes and rich fragrances. True lilies belong to the genus Lilium, representing an incredibly diverse group of plants that hold deep symbolic meaning across various global cultures.

Cultural Symbology

Universally celebrated for their elegant appearance, lilies are deeply rooted in history. White lilies, particularly Madonna lilies, have historically symbolized purity, virtue, and renewal. In contrast, vibrant orange tiger lilies often represent passion, confidence, and prosperity, making them a popular choice for dynamic floral arrangements.

Botanical Structure

Unlike many other garden flowers, true lilies grow from underground bulbs that store nutrients for winter dormancy. They feature narrow, lance-shaped leaves arranged in whorls along a singular, strong stem. At the peak of the stem, they develop large, multi-petaled blossoms displaying distinct stamens coated in heavy pollen.

Care & Growing Conditions

Lilies thrive best when planted in a location that offers "heads in the sun, feet in the shade." This means their foliage and blossoms benefit from full or partial sunlight, while their root systems prefer rich, organic soil kept cool with a layer of mulch. Good drainage is absolutely crucial to keep the bulbs healthy year after year.
